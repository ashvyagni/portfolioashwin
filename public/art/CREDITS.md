# Artwork

All artwork here is from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection,
released into the public domain (CC0). Figures were cut out from their backgrounds;
images were cropped and resized.

- `yamuna.jpg` — "Krishna and the Gopis on the Bank of the Yamuna River", folio from the
  "Second Guler-Kangra Bhagavata Purana", India, ca. 1775–80. Gift of Steven Kossak,
  The Kronos Collections. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/765654
- `gandharva.png` — Celestial Musician (Gandharva), India (Karnataka), 11th century.
  Gift of Florence and Herbert Irving. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38142
- `attendant.png` — Celestial Attendant, India, ca. late 10th century. Anonymous Gift, 1996.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/39608
- `kalpasutra.jpg` — Celestial Performers: Folios from a Kalpasutra Manuscript, India
  (Gujarat), ca. 1490. Purchase, Margery and Harry Kahn Gift, 1977.
  https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/37836
